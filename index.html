Twelve essays and studies, from building baseband silicon and applying its discipline to where AI inference is heading. One lens throughout: the arithmetic is nearly free and the bytes are the bill — so move only the data that is needed, in as few bits as possible, drop the least valuable first under strain, and on a limited fabric prioritize by cost against return. Every modeled number states its method and its bounds; the pages with live simulations run in the browser, and the code is in the repo.
